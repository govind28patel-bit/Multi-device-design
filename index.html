Context · Smart mobility

The car stopped being a machine you drive and became a platform you stay connected to.

Smart mobility moves the focus from the single vehicle to an ecosystem that links vehicles, infrastructure and people. That shift is what makes a multi-device experience necessary in the first place.

1900 – 2010

ANALOG

Combustion engines, manual control, disconnected hardware and linear ownership.

2024 – BEYOND

DIGITAL

Electric drivetrains, AI-assisted driving, V2X connectivity and mobility as a service.

SHIFT

Traditional vehicles

  • Mechanical focusValue comes from hardware, powertrain and build quality. Performance stays the same for the car's whole life.
  • Isolated operationInformation silos, with no real-time link to infrastructure or other vehicles.
  • Fixed functionalityFeatures are locked at the point of sale. Improvements need new parts or a dealership visit.

Smart mobility

  • Software-defined (SDV)Intelligence lives in software. Hardware becomes a platform for over-the-air updates and services.
  • Hyper-connectedV2X lets the car exchange data with the cloud, traffic lights and pedestrians.
  • Autonomous-readySensors and AI that can grow from driver assistance towards self-driving through updates.

How it works

What happens when you tap “Lock” from 2 km away

Before designing a single screen, I mapped how the phone actually reaches a parked car. Every backstage row in the blueprint below follows this path.

Your phoneMe4U app
Internet4G / 5G / Wi-Fi
Mahindra CloudMe4U platform
Car's 5G moduleAlways on, even when locked
Car systemsLocks, battery, climate, lights
  1. The app sends the request over 4G, 5G or Wi-Fi to Mahindra's cloud.
  2. The cloud confirms you are the authorised owner.
  3. It sends the command to your specific car using its unique identifier.
  4. The car's 5G module receives it and wakes the systems it needs.
  5. The car replies with current data: battery level, location, lock state.
  6. The response travels back along the same path to your phone.

Service blueprint

Two days, three devices

I tested the idea against two realistic journeys. Each blueprint shows what the owner does, what each device shows, and what happens backstage in the cloud and the car.

Scenario

Scroll the blueprint sideways on small screens. Press the arrow to switch scenarios.

User flow

Kept shallow on purpose

Every task in both journeys is reachable within two taps of Home: navigation and battery status from the home screen, and sending a route to the car straight from search.

Phone → Car → Watch

Morning · at home · phone

Home is a command centre

The first thing you see when you open Me4U. It gives a full snapshot of the XEV 9S at a glance, range, charge and lock state, without extra taps or navigation.

Morning · at home · phone

Plan the route before leaving the bed
